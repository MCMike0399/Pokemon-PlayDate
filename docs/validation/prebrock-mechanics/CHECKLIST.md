# Pallet → Brock mechanics closure inventory

This is a source review, **not milestone acceptance**. The runnable seen/Focus candidate is separate and byte-frozen. `prebrock-checklist.json` pins the actual production files reviewed, retains every one of the 417 level-up events from all 30 technical-scope species, and records all 135 distinct moves with their current production definition and independent pinned Showdown body locations. `moves-checklist.md` is the readable move index. `path-present` means a production path exists and no bespoke gap was identified here; it does not mean exhaustive per-move SDK proof. No stale `content-gaps.json` report is used.

## Actual availability, without a grinding cutoff

The 14 direct species are three selectable starters, seven catchable wild species (Rattata/Pidgey/Caterpie/Metapod/Weedle/Kakuna/Pikachu), and four trainer-only species (Diglett/Sandshrew/Geodude/Onix). The 30-species technical evolution closure is retained in full. That closure is not an acquisition claim.

Across starter choices, 21 species are player-obtainable through existing catches and ordinary level evolution before Brock; a single starter campaign has 15. Each campaign includes its chosen starter's three stages, the two bug lines, Pidgey's three stages, Rattata's two stages and Pikachu. Level evolution can be delayed or cancelled, so base-species later moves remain required. Every source level-up event through 100 is listed; level16 is only a normal-route review priority marker, never a cap or waiver.

Raichu remains in the technical closure, but no pre-Brock Thunder Stone acquisition exists. Diglett/Sandshrew/Geodude/Onix and their evolved families remain trainer-only in this connected slice; neither their hypothetical level100 moves nor Golem's trade hook are claimed as naturally player-obtainable. Evolved-species level1 moves earlier than the species' earliest obtainable level are explicitly flagged for inheritance/relearner review, rather than pretending a constructor projection gives those moves to a naturally evolved specimen. Caught Metapod/Kakuna can legitimately exist below their evolution thresholds.

Original Route22 is a **world/content gap**. It remains required and must not disappear from the campaign by defining the current connection boundary as complete. Pinned Blue slots include Rattata, Nidoran F, Spearow and Nidoran M, levels2–5. The optional first rival has Pidgey9 plus the counter-starter8. Adding this actual map requires its separate reachable species/evolution/move closure, Poison Point/Rivalry (including proper sex provenance), source rival and event acceptance. These are not waived by the current 14/30 inventory.
